SI imports dicts/zi.dict.yaml, dicts/jichu.dict.yaml,
dicts/duoyin.dict.yaml and dicts/en.dict.yaml into base.csv.
The other normal Chinese sources remain separate CSV categories named after the source files:
diming, fangyan, huaxue, mingren, shici, taifeng, wuzhong, yaopin, yiren, yixue.
The cuoyin, abbreviation, T9 abbreviation and mixed-language tables are excluded.
Weights are retained without wordfreq normalization or frequency rescaling.
SI removes Chinese tone marks, maps ü to v, and merges tone-collapsed
text/reading pairs by maximum weight. English uses SI full-text typing keys;
case-only duplicates use the highest-weight spelling, with first-source ties.
Missing weights are stored as zero. The pinned source's integer+N/integer+s
weights use their numeric prefix, matching Rime's numeric parser.
Private-use outputs and unsupported English forms are excluded and reported.

Local SI category texts/readings are retained; their weights were set to 10
on October 29, 2026. New local entries default to 10. The base owns overlapping weights
and English casing. Imported Wanxiang categories retain upstream weights over
legacy category copies; preserved categories supplement other identities.
Historical MSIME, SCOWL, CSpell, Rime Ice and wordfreq notices remain with
the distribution for preserved category data and source history.

No upstream program code, grammar model or runtime is imported by this change.
SI's independently written conversion script and application remain GPLv3.
Full upstream LICENSE-SCOPE.md and NOTICE.md, source checksums and conversion
report are retained under Data/Vocabulary/Wanxiang in the source distribution.
No user input, private phrases or learning archives are dictionary inputs.
