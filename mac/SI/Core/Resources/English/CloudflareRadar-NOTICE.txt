The source is an unordered top-1000 bucket based on DNS queries to Cloudflare's
1.1.1.1 resolver, covering seven days and updated weekly. The source's bucket
membership does not provide an individual numerical rank or input-method word
frequency, and is not an endorsement or security/availability assessment.

SI validates full lowercase ASCII domains, retains dots/hyphens/digits as literal
readings, and merges exact duplicate domains without changing existing weights.
New entries use an editorial weight of 100. SI's separately authored domain
selection is retained alongside the imported data. Snapshot bytes, retrieval time,
checksums and transformations are recorded in Data/Vocabulary/meta.json.
